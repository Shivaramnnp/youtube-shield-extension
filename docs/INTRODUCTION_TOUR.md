## 1. The Quick-Access Shield HUD

Whenever you open YouTube, you will notice the glowing **`Shield 🛡️`** button in the top navigation masthead (next to the Create button and your profile).

### What’s Inside the HUD Popover:
* **Master Extension Switch:** Turn all YouTube Shield features ON or OFF with a single click.
* **Instant Mode Toggles:** One-click activation for *Shorts Blocker*, *Study Mode*, *Goal Mode*, *Clean UI*, and *Auto Skip Ads*.
* **Live Session Monitor:** Displays your current active watch time and learning score.
* **Mini Audio Station:** Adjust Volume Boost (100%–600%), Bass Boost (+0dB to +20dB), and switch EQ Presets on the fly without leaving your video.
* **Dashboard Shortcut:** Direct one-click access to the full Options & Analytics Dashboard.

---

## 2. Shorts Blocker (Total Shorts Elimination)

Shorts Blocker completely sanitizes YouTube from short-form dopamine loops across the entire site.

* **Sidebar & Drawer Removal:** Eliminates the "Shorts" tab from the left sidebar and mobile drawer.
* **Feed Elimination:** Removes Shorts rows and shelves from your Home Feed, Subscriptions, Search Results, and Channel pages.
* **Smart URL Interception:** If you click a direct link to `youtube.com/shorts/...`, YouTube Shield instantly intercepts the navigation and redirects you back to safety without jarring page reloads.
* **Gamified Counter:** Every blocked Short adds to your **Shorts Destroyed** statistic and earns you Action Points!

## 3. Study Mode & Integrated Pomodoro Timer

Transform YouTube into a high-yield learning workstation.

* **Top Status Banner:** A sleek, non-intrusive floating banner at the top of the video player keeps you anchored to your learning session.
* **Integrated Pomodoro Engine:**
  * **25-minute Work Cycles:** Dedicated focus blocks.
  * **5-minute Short Breaks & 15-minute Long Breaks:** Rest intervals.
  * **Interactive Controls:** Pause, play, skip, or reset cycles directly from the top banner.
  * **Audio Chimes:** Subtle futuristic audio alerts signal break and work transitions.
* **Smart Content Validation:** Automatically detects if a video matches educational topics. If you wander into gaming or clickbait, an **Alignment Warning** gently prompts you to stay on track.
* **Learning Time Conversion:** All time spent in Study Mode accumulates toward your **Learning Rank** and unlocks scholar badges.

---

## 4. Goal Mode (Intentional Browsing)

Goal Mode stops mindless auto-scrolling by holding you accountable to a specific intent.

* **Set Your Intent:** Set a specific objective (e.g. *"Learn React Server Components"* or *"Prepare for Python Interview"*).
* **Defensive Overlay Guard:** If you open a video that doesn't match your goal, the player is safely paused and a frosted-glass **Goal Block Overlay** appears.
* **Allow Once Override:** If a video is an edge case you genuinely need to watch, click **"Allow Once"** to permit that video while keeping Goal Mode active for everything else.

---

## 5. Feed Controller (AI Keyword Filtering)

Take control of YouTube’s recommendation algorithm.

* **Whitelist Keywords:** Enter keywords for topics you care about (e.g. `javascript, coding, astronomy, philosophy`).
* **Home & Search Cleansing:** Videos that do not match your whitelist or contain your blocked keywords are automatically filtered out.
* **High-Signal Browsing:** Replaces the chaotic home feed with a curated library of high-value videos.

## 6. UI Cleaner (Distraction-Free Zen Interface)

Strip away visual clutter and vanity metrics.

* **Notification Bell:** Hide the distracting red notification badge.
* **Subscriber Counts:** Hide subscriber and view counts to judge content by merit rather than social proof.
* **Live Chat:** Automatically collapses live stream chat boxes.
* **Explore & Trending:** Hides the gossip/trending tabs.
* **Autoplay Switch:** Disables YouTube’s automatic continuous playback.
* **Comments Section:** Toggle comments on or off to prevent comment-section rabbit holes.

## 7. Auto Ad Skipper & Playback Assurance

Zero-latency, native ad skipping powered by MAIN-world execution.

* **Native MAIN-World Injection:** Bypasses isolated-world synthetic click restrictions by executing directly within the page context.
* **16x Speedup Engine:** Unskippable ads and countdown timers are accelerated to 16x speed and muted, ending in milliseconds.
* **Instant Skip Trigger:** Clicks the `Skip ⏭️` button the millisecond it becomes active.
* **Active Playback Assurance:** Auto-resumes `video.play()` if YouTube pauses on stream transitions, guaranteeing uninterrupted video playback.
* **Anti-Adblock Dismissal:** Automatically closes enforcement popups without corrupting native YouTube menus.

## 8. Audio Engine, Volume Booster & 10-Band Graphic EQ

Studio-quality Web Audio API sound processing built directly into YouTube.

* **600% Volume Booster:** Boost quiet audio tracks, podcasts, and lecture recordings up to 6x normal volume with zero distortion clipping.
* **Dedicated Bass Subsystem:** Add up to **+20dB** of punchy low-end punch via high-order low-shelf filtering.
* **10-Band Graphic Equalizer:** Precision sliders spanning **32Hz, 64Hz, 125Hz, 250Hz, 500Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz, 4kHz, 8kHz, and 16kHz** with a ±12dB gain range.
* **8 Curated Presets:**
  * 🎵 **Flat** (Reference standard)
  * 🔊 **Bass Boost** (Punchy sub-bass)
  * 🎙️ **Vocal Booster** (Enhanced dialogue clarity for podcasts/lectures)
  * 🎸 **Rock & Pop** (Dynamic midrange and punch)
  * 🎻 **Acoustic** (Warm instrument separation)
  * ⚡ **Electronic** (Deep bass + crisp highs)
  * 🛠️ **Custom** (Save your personalized curve)

## 9. Time Manager & Daily Limit Schedules

Reclaim your time with hard boundaries and scheduled access.

* **Daily Watch Time Limit:** Set a maximum allowance per day (e.g. 45 minutes).
* **Schedule Blocking:** Lock YouTube during work/study hours (e.g. 09:00 to 17:00).
* **Focus Check-in Reminders:** Gentle full-screen check-ins every 15, 30, or 60 minutes asking: *"Are you still watching intentionally?"*
* **Emergency Snooze:** If you hit your daily limit during an urgent task, use the **+5 Minute Snooze** button.
